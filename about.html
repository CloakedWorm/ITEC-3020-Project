About CloverField

Built by our founder Dofe Lore(1952-1992), CloverField Technologies started out as a small lab running out a garage. At first this lab was under utilized by the Mr.Lore, only being used for personal research and project, but one day Mr.Lore would have a desire to change the world, one gene at a time. for the next 30 years he would dedicate his life to lead research in genetics and pharmaceuticals and due to his inovations, CloverField Technologies is now a forerunning in chemical engineering, R&D and manufacturing. After passing on his passion to his daughters: Alex and Mai, the company expanded their endevors to branch out in the private sectors.

Our Mission
